The texture collection is based on planetary imagery and elevation products, but its publisher notes that some unmapped areas are filled with fictional terrain and that colors are enhanced. These textures are used only to render an illustrative surface for orientation. They are not scientific analysis products or measurements, and do not establish elevation, slope, composition, or other site properties.
