# Project overview

**Purpose:** Set the product mission, target users, core user journey and boundaries for Terracroma.ai.

## Scope
### In scope
- Challenge-aligned discovery of new Earth analog candidates for selected Moon/Mars regions
- Evidence-backed characterization, partial transferability and field-test planning
- A hackathon demonstrator built around a small curated region/target pair

### Out of scope
- Full mission-design suite, global high-resolution ingest, validated hazard/safety certification, XR, production accounts

### Product thesis
Terracroma.ai helps mission and field-science teams find Earth locations that reproduce selected environmental or terrain conditions of lunar and Martian sites, then shows where the analogy stops. It is a decision-support research tool, not a landing-site authority, certified safety product or substitute for domain experts.

### Primary journey
Choose a Moon/Mars target and experiment profile; inspect the target signature; scan a manageable Earth candidate set; inspect ranked matches with evidence and coverage; compare non-transferable dimensions; assemble complementary sites if needed; export a reproducible experiment brief.

### User groups
Planetary geologists; analog-site researchers; rover and field robotics teams; mission planners; students and educators exploring open planetary data.
