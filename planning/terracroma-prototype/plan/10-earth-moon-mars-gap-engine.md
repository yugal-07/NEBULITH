# Earth–Moon–Mars Gap Engine

**Purpose:** Make irreducible planetary differences visible and convert them into a practical testing/mitigation plan.

## Scope
### In scope
- Gravity, atmospheric pressure/composition, radiation, thermal cycling, dust, illumination and terrain conditions
- Transferability categories and facility interventions

### Out of scope
- Claiming field analogs reproduce vacuum, gravity or planetary radiation
- Combining qualitative statements into a numeric score without evidence
