# Core ideas and novelty strategy

**Purpose:** Preserve the submitted Terracroma.ai baseline while selecting a coherent set of additional capabilities that create a defensible research and mission-planning identity.

## Scope
### In scope
- Existing 9-module feature baseline
- Novelty portfolio, differentiation matrix and MVP decisions
- A coherent Discover → Explain → Assemble → Simulate → Optimize workflow

### Out of scope
- Building every proposed idea in the hackathon
- Calling an idea globally unique without prior-art review

### Ranked differentiators
| Proposal | Classification vs baseline | Data/approach | Demo value | Caveat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Analog Transferability Intelligence | Novel | Condition-level transfer labels plus gap matrix and intervention suggestions | Shows exactly what an Earth campaign can validate | Expert-reviewed rule set; never equate sites |
| Multi-Site Analog Network | Novel | Set-cover/coverage optimization across sites and experiments | One target, several complementary Earth sites | Coverage depends on explicit experiment definitions |
| Planetary Time Machine | Extension → novel when stability metric is added | HLS/Landsat time series; seasonal stability and campaign windows | Playback and stability evidence | Earth and Mars seasons are not interchangeable; compare selected proxies only |
| Experiment Coverage Optimizer | Novel | Bipartite experiment-condition-site coverage graph; weighted set cover | Suggest site combination that covers more tests | Weights are user-defined or cited, not learned from no ground truth |
| Mission Failure Prediction Lab | Extension | Terrain slope/roughness/obstacle indicators and configurable rover envelope | Route stress test and explainable warnings | Hazard indicators, not certified safety |
| Field Campaign Intelligence | Novel | Information gain / data gap ranking; survey transect planner | Shows next measurement most likely to reduce uncertainty | Recommendations are decision support, not field permits |
| Autonomous Analog Discovery Agent | Extension to existing RAG + discovery | Tool-calling workflow with structured claims, retrieval and human approval | Agent produces auditable candidate dossier | LLM never supplies measurements or bypasses deterministic scoring |
| Analog Degradation Monitor | Novel | Longitudinal trends in imagery, moisture, snow, vegetation and thermal products | Candidate trend timeline | Time series availability and sensor harmonization vary |
| Counterfactual Mission Lab | Novel | Sensitivity/what-if simulation of weights, rover parameters and route limits | Interactive parameter changes move recommendations | Simulation simplifications must be visible |
| Human Factors & Habitat Stress Lab | Future | Illumination, environmental workload proxies and logistics | Habitat rehearsal context | Avoid medical or physiological risk conclusions |

### Hackathon MVP signature set
A. Explainable analog discovery using one carefully bounded target and AOI. B. Transferability and irreducible-gap panel. C. Multi-site experiment coverage with a small manually curated experiment catalog. D. A short terrain route rehearsal with evidence-based slope/roughness alerts. E. A short time-series stability sparkline only if compatible scenes are available.

### Do not build for the 24–48 hour event
- Unverified global high-resolution scan of every Earth pixel.
- “AI predicts life” or “AI predicts mission success.”
- Real-time radiation or astronaut medical safety inference from surface imagery.
- A generic chatbot that answers without page-level citations.
- Full planetary physics, realistic microgravity, vacuum or radiation simulation.
- XR/VR unless the required core workflow is already stable.
- Fake live telemetry, fake users, fake performance metrics or fabricated field results.
